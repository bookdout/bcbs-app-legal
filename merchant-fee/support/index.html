Merchant Fee Calculator Support

Merchant Fee Calculator is an offline utility for calculating effective card-processing rates, comparing alternative pricing plans, and reviewing saved fee history.

Using the calculator

Enter the total card volume and total processing fees from the same monthly statement. Transaction count is optional, but is required when comparing a plan that includes a per-transaction fee.

Comparisons and estimates

Plan comparisons and break-even results are estimates based on the numbers you enter. Actual processing costs can vary with card mix, transaction size, and provider terms.

Saved reviews

Saved reviews remain on your device. The app has no account or cloud backup, so removing the app also removes its saved history.
