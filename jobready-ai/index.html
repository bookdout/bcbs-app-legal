Privacy Policy — JobReady Ai: Resume Builder

Effective date: 2026-08-01

App: JobReady Ai: Resume Builder (iOS) · Publisher: Bull Creek Business Solutions ("we", "us") · Contact: caleb.krantz@hotmail.com

The short version

Your résumé, profile, application tracker, and generated documents live on your device. We do not run accounts, ads, or analytics. In the current release, nothing you type or import ever leaves your phone.

What the app stores (on your device only)

  • Your profile: contact details, work experience, education, skills, certifications
  • Imported résumé files are parsed on-device; the extracted text becomes your editable profile
  • Job descriptions you paste
  • Generated tailored résumés, cover letters, and interview briefs
  • Your application tracker (companies, roles, statuses, dates, notes)
  • A local count of free/purchased package credits

This data is stored in the app's local storage. Deleting the app deletes it. We have no copy.

What we collect

Current release (offline assistant): nothing. All analysis and tailoring runs on-device. The app makes no network requests containing your content.

Future AI mode: a later update may offer cloud AI tailoring. If enabled, your profile text and the pasted job description would be sent, over an encrypted connection, to a server we operate, solely to generate your gap summary and tailored documents, and passed to an AI provider (Anthropic or OpenAI) under API terms that do not use the data to train models. We would not store your résumé content on the server — only anonymous usage counts (token totals) for cost monitoring. If this changes the app's privacy posture, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before release.

Purchases

Subscriptions and packs are processed by Apple. We receive no payment card details. Your entitlement (e.g., remaining package credits) is stored on your device.

Children

JobReady Ai is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete any profile data in the app at any time
  • Delete the app to remove all locally stored data
  • Manage subscriptions in your App Store account settings

Changes

We will update this policy here and note material changes in App Store release notes.
