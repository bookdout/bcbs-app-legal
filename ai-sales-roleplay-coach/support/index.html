Frequently Asked

What are the starter objections?

New installs come with a library of 50 researched objections, clearly badged "Starter", so you can practice immediately. They are placeholders for your real field experience — edit each one into the wording you actually hear, or delete the ones you never encounter. Objections you capture yourself become your own drills.

What does the free version include?

Everything local is free forever: capturing objections, the fact library, the offline practice buyer, feedback history, and spaced repetition. You also get 3 free cloud AI roleplays. A subscription ($9.99/month or $49.99/year) adds 30 adaptive cloud AI roleplays each month.

Why does the buyer keep pushing back?

By design: instant rebuttals are never rewarded. Acknowledge the concern and ask a genuine question first — the buyer opens up when you earn it, exactly like the field.

Where is my data?

Your objections, facts, and practice history live on your device. Cloud roleplays send only the current scenario and conversation text to our server to generate the buyer's replies — see the privacy policy.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open the Coach Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID.
