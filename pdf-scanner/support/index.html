Frequently Asked

Does the app make up experience I don't have?

No — that's the point. Every rewritten bullet is tied to a real bullet from your résumé ("Based on:" is shown for each line), skills you don't have never appear, and genuine gaps are called out so you can prepare for them instead of hiding them.

Why does the assistant say "offline mode"?

The current version tailors your documents entirely on your device with a deterministic assistant, clearly labeled in-app. Nothing you import or type leaves your phone. A future update may add cloud AI tailoring — the app and our privacy policy will say so clearly before that ships.

What does the free tier include?

Profile setup, unlimited ATS keyword analyses, and 1 full tailored application package (résumé + cover letter + interview brief). AI PDF Scanner Pro removes the limit: $9.99/month or $49.99/year, or a one-time 5-package pack for $7.99.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Manage or cancel any time in Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions.

My PDF didn't import correctly.

Scanned or image-based PDFs can't be read as text on-device; the app detects this and routes you to guided manual entry. Digitally-created PDF and DOCX résumés import best.
