Privacy Policy — InkGuru Ai

Effective date: 2026-08-01

App: InkGuru Ai (iOS)

Publisher: Bull Creek Business Solutions

Contact: caleb.krantz@hotmail.com

Summary

InkGuru Ai turns written tattoo ideas into visual design concepts, lets you preview placement on a body photo, and exports stencil-style line art. In the current version (preview mode) everything happens entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

Information We Collect

None. InkGuru Ai does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, contacts, or location, and does not include analytics or advertising SDKs. In preview mode the app makes no network connections at all.

Body Photos

Your body photos never leave your device — in every version of the app. Placement preview and stencil export are computed entirely on your iPhone. Photos you choose are read from your photo library only to render the preview; the app does not copy them into its own storage and never uploads them.

What the App Stores (on your device only)

Design briefs you write (subject, style, line weight, color preference); generated design images, favorites, and history; your remaining trial/credit balance and opaque purchase transaction identifiers. Deleting a design set in History deletes its images. Deleting the app deletes everything.

When Cloud Generation Ships

A future update will add true AI generation through a server operated by Bull Creek. When that mode is active, your text prompt and an anonymous per-install identifier (random, not linked to your identity) will be sent over an encrypted connection to our server, used only to create your designs, and not used to train AI models. Body photos will still never leave your device. The app will clearly indicate when cloud mode is active, and this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated (with a new effective date) before that feature launches.

Purchases

Subscriptions and credit packs are processed by Apple In-App Purchase. We receive no card numbers and no billing address — only an anonymized receipt that unlocks your credits on-device. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data, because we do not collect any. Images you save or share go only to the destination you pick.

Children's Privacy

InkGuru Ai is not directed at children and is inappropriate for users under the app's App Store age rating. Because it collects no data, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Deletion and Your Rights

Everything lives on your device, so deleting the app is complete deletion. Once cloud generation exists, you may email us to delete any server-side job records tied to your anonymous identifier. Depending on your region (GDPR, CCPA, and similar), you may also have rights to access and portability — the same contact honors those requests.

Health & Safety Note

InkGuru Ai provides design inspiration only. It does not provide medical, dermatological, or skin-placement advice. A professional tattoo artist must adapt and approve any design — and confirm safe placement — before it is tattooed.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the revised policy at the same URL.
