Frequently Asked

Why do my designs say "Preview mode"?

The current version runs entirely on your device and produces clearly labeled placeholder designs while our cloud generation service rolls out. Styles, placement preview, stencil export, and history all work fully offline. The app will tell you exactly when cloud generation is active.

What do the plans include?

Free: 2 trial generation jobs. Monthly ($9.99) or Yearly ($59.99) subscriptions include 30 generation jobs every month. The 5-job credit pack ($4.99) never expires. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in your App Store settings.

Where are my photos and designs stored?

On your device. Body photos never leave your iPhone in any version of the app. Designs, favorites, and history live in the app's local storage; deleting a set in History removes it permanently.

How do I use a design for a real tattoo?

Export the stencil-style version and bring it to a professional tattoo artist. Designs are inspiration and reference material only — your artist must adapt and approve any design and confirm safe placement.
