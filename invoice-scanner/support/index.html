AI Invoice Scanner Support

AI Invoice Scanner & Tracker is an offline invoice capture, organization, and export app from Bull Creek Business Solutions.

Camera access

The app uses the camera only when you choose to photograph an invoice. If access was previously denied, open iOS Settings, select AI Invoice Scanner, and enable Camera.

Exports and backups

Use the app's export tools to create PDF or CSV files, then choose a destination in the iOS share sheet. App records are stored locally, so export important records before deleting the app or changing devices.

Contact support

Email caleb@bullcreekbusinesssolutions.io. Please include your device model, iOS version, and a description of the problem. Do not email invoices or sensitive financial information.
