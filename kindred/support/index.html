Kindred Support

Kindred is a couples card game from Bull Creek Business Solutions.

Getting help

If the app is not working as expected, first close and reopen it and confirm that iOS is up to date. If the problem continues, email caleb@bullcreekbusinesssolutions.io.

Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not include private or sensitive information.

Purchases

The initial App Store version is free and does not require a purchase. If purchases are added in a future version, restoration and billing support instructions will be provided here.

Privacy

Kindred does not require an account. Game activity stays on your device.
