Privacy Policy — PhotoGuru Ai

Effective date: 2026-08-01

App: PhotoGuru Ai (iOS)

Publisher: Bull Creek Business Solutions

Contact: caleb@bullcreekbusinesssolutions.io

The short version

PhotoGuru Ai collects no data. None. Every scan and every decision happens on your device. Your photos and videos never leave your phone.

What PhotoGuru Ai does with your photo library

PhotoGuru Ai reads your photo library — with your permission — solely to find duplicates, visually similar photos, screenshots, blurry photos and large videos, and to show you how much storage you could recover. All of this analysis runs on your device using Apple's PhotoKit. PhotoGuru Ai:

  • has no servers and makes no network calls;
  • requires no account and has no sign-in;
  • contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind;
  • never uploads photos, videos, thumbnails, or any information derived from them;
  • never deletes anything without your explicit confirmation — and even then, iOS shows its own system confirmation and moves items to the Photos app's Recently Deleted album, where they remain recoverable for about 30 days.

Data stored on your device

PhotoGuru Ai keeps a small amount of data locally on your device only: your cleanup history (dates, item counts, and estimated sizes — never photo content), and a cached record of your purchases so your unlock works offline. Deleting the app deletes this data. We never see it.

Purchases

Purchases (the one-time cleanup unlock and the optional Smart Categories subscription) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's own privacy policy.

Permissions

Photo library: required to scan and (only with your confirmation) clean up items. PhotoGuru Ai fully supports iOS "Selected Photos" limited access if you prefer to expose only part of your library.

Children's Privacy

PhotoGuru Ai is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above (for example, optional cloud features), this policy will be updated first — with a new effective date at this same URL — and the App Store privacy label will be revised before that version ships.
