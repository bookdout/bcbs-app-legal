Frequently Asked

Is scanning really free?

Yes. Scanning your library and reviewing everything the app finds is free, with no caps and no account. The one-time $19.99 unlock is only for the final cleanup (delete) action. The optional $14.99/year Smart Categories subscription adds one-tap smart selections and also includes the cleanup action while it is active.

Where do my photos go?

Nowhere. Every scan and comparison runs on your iPhone. PhotoGuru Ai has no servers and makes no network calls — photos, thumbnails, and results never leave your device.

What happens when I clean up?

You select items, the app shows a storage-recovery estimate and asks you to confirm, and then iOS shows its own system confirmation before moving items to the Photos app's Recently Deleted album. They stay recoverable there for about 30 days.

Something looks wrongly grouped

"Similar", "likely best" and "possibly blurry" are conservative on-device heuristics — the final call is always yours; nothing is ever selected or deleted automatically. If you see a bad grouping, we'd love a report by email.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchases. Your unlock is tied to your Apple ID.
